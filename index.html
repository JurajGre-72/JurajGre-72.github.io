Reelwright

Automated short-form video

Scripts in. Voiced, captioned, scored vertical video out.

Reelwright is a content pipeline that turns short fact-based scripts into fully produced vertical videos — neural voiceover, word-synced kinetic captions, licensed background footage, and an original backing track — then publishes the result straight to TikTok through the official Content Posting API.

01What it does

Voiceover

Neural text-to-speech narration generated per script, with word-level timing extracted for caption sync.

Captions

Word-by-word kinetic captions that highlight in sync with the voiceover, styled for vertical viewing.

Background

Licensed stock footage, cropped and looped to length, composited behind the caption layer.

Music

An original synthesized backing track mixed under the narration at a level that stays out of the way.

02Sample output

demo_video.mp4 — end-to-end render, TikTok Login Kit + Content Posting API integration

03How it connects to TikTok

  1. The operator authorizes Reelwright once via TikTok's official OAuth login (Login Kit), scoped to their own account.
  2. Reelwright renders the finished video locally from a script in its content queue.
  3. The finished file is uploaded through TikTok's Content Posting API, landing in the connected account's inbox as a draft.
  4. The operator reviews the draft in the TikTok app and posts it.

04Who operates it

Reelwright is a single-operator tool: it renders and posts video for the one TikTok account of the person running it. It has no public sign-up, no other users, and no dashboard for third parties — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what data it touches and where it goes.
