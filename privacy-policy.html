Privacy Policy

How Reelwright handles data

Reelwright is a single-operator content automation tool. It is not offered as a public service, and it does not collect data from anyone other than its own operator.

01What Reelwright is

Reelwright is a personal pipeline that generates short-form video (voiceover, captions, and background visuals) and publishes it to one TikTok account — the account belonging to the person who runs it. It runs on the operator's own machine and is not a hosted app, a website with visitors, or a tool used by third parties.

02Data this tool handles

  • TikTok OAuth tokens. When the operator connects their TikTok account via TikTok's official OAuth flow, Reelwright receives an access token and refresh token scoped to that one account.
  • Generated media. Video, audio, and caption text produced locally by the operator's own scripts, from content the operator authored.
  • Nothing from anyone else. Reelwright has no visitors, no accounts other than the operator's, and no sign-up flow of its own.

03Where data is stored

OAuth tokens are written to a local file on the operator's own device, outside of any web-accessible directory. They are never transmitted anywhere except to open.tiktokapis.com, the official TikTok API host, in order to authenticate posting requests.

Reelwright does not operate a database, does not use analytics or tracking cookies, and does not share, sell, or otherwise transmit data to any party besides TikTok's own API.

04Third-party services

The only third-party service Reelwright communicates with is TikTok's Content Posting API. No other analytics, advertising, or data-processing service is integrated.

05Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept locally until the operator deletes the local token file or revokes the connection from within TikTok's own account settings (Settings → Security → Manage Account Access). Revoking access there immediately invalidates Reelwright's ability to post.

06Changes to this policy

This policy is updated as Reelwright's functionality changes. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
